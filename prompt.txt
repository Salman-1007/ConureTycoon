Recombination & Crossover Mechanics (Pineapple Locus)Because Cinnamon (CIN) and Yellow-Sided (OP) both reside on the avian $Z$ chromosome, they do not sort independently.Model genetic linkage with a recombinant crossover rate:$$r = 0.25 \quad (25\% \text{ chance of crossing over during meiosis in heterozygous males } Z^{cin, +} / Z^{+, op})$$Coupling (Cis) phase ($Z^{cin, op} / Z^{+, +}$): Recombination separates the linked traits into single-mutation gametes ($Z^{cin}$ or $Z^{op}$) at rate $r$.Repulsion (Trans) phase ($Z^{cin, +} / Z^{+, op}$): Recombination creates wild-type ($Z^{+, +}$) or dual-mutant ($Z^{cin, op}$ - Pineapple) gametes at rate $r$.4.
